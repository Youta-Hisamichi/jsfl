Smash enemies with the Reflection Orb — it gets stronger every time it bounces!
An idle hack & slash RPG where your adventure keeps going even while you're away.

■ The more it bounces, the stronger it gets
Tackle the Reflection Orb to send it flying. Each bounce off a wall or obstacle raises its multiplier.
Build it up, then crash it into the enemy horde for one massive hit.
Upgrade how fast the multiplier grows and how hard your tackle hits with shop items.

■ Progress even while idle
Your hero fights automatically and keeps pushing through the stages.
Coins pile up while the app is closed — come back to collect your Welcome Back bonus.

■ Recruit a colorful party
Summon and train companions from N to UR rarity.
Their "Bond" and "Grace" abilities make the whole party stronger.

■ Rebirth and go further
Stuck? Rebirth! Keep permanent bonuses and SSR+ treasure chests, then dive into deeper stages.

■ Mighty bosses and the Tower of Trials
Powerful bosses wait at every milestone. Master skills like Meteor and Big Bang to break through.
Test your strength in the Tower of Trials.

■ Climb the rankings
Compete in daily clears, best stage, max reflection multiplier and more.
