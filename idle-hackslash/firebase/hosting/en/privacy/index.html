Privacy Policy

Game app "Mugen Hansha: Idle Hack RPG" (the "App")

The developer of the App (the "Developer") handles information about the people who play the App ("Users") as described below.

1. Information we collect and how we use it

Game save dataYour progress, such as stages, companions and items. It is stored on your device, and also on Firebase (below) as a backup and for moving to a new device.
Anonymous user IDWhen you open the App, Firebase Authentication automatically creates an anonymous ID with no name. It is used only to tell your save backup apart from other people's. It does not include your name or email address.
Google / Apple account information (only if you use it)If you link your Google or Apple account under "Device Transfer" in the settings, Firebase Authentication stores that account's ID, email address and display name. It is used only to load the same save on a new device.
Advertising informationThe App shows video ads. The ad network (Google AdMob) collects information such as the advertising ID, device type, OS version and IP address, and uses it to show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud.
Purchase recordsPayments for paid items and subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play and the App Store. The Developer never receives payment details such as credit card numbers. Purchase records (which product was bought) are used only to apply the purchase in the game.
Feedback you sendIf you send feedback from the in-game rating dialog, the text, the app version, your platform and your game progress are stored on Firebase so that the Developer can read it and improve the App.
Connection logsTo check bonus times correctly, the App asks a server for the current time. As with any normal connection, the server may record your IP address and similar information.

The Developer does not collect your name, address, phone number, precise location, contacts or photos.

2. Third-party services

The following services handle information according to their own policies.

  • Firebase (Google LLC): https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The servers of these services may be located outside your country (for example, in the United States), and information may be stored and processed there.

3. Ad settings

You can reset your advertising ID or turn off interest-based (personalized) ads in your device settings. On Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (the names may differ slightly by device). On iPhone, the App asks for permission to track for advertising; you can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Sharing with third parties

The Developer does not sell the information collected. Except for passing it to the services above and when required by law, the Developer does not share it with third parties.

5. Deleting your data and account

  • Save data on your device is deleted when you delete the App.
  • Delete in the App: Tap "🗑 Delete cloud data" in the settings to immediately delete the save backup on Firebase, your anonymous ID and any linked account information (sign-in records). After that, nothing is stored in the cloud (the save on your device remains).
  • Request by email: If you cannot delete it in the App (for example, because you already deleted the App), contact us at the address below with the subject "Data deletion request". We may ask for the information shown under "Device Transfer" to confirm your data. We will delete it within 30 days.

6. Children

The App is not directed primarily at children under 13. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may change. Important changes will be announced in the App's news or on this page.
