Mugen Hansha
Idle Hack RPG

Send the Reflection Orb flying — it gets stronger with every bounce — and wipe out whole enemy hordes.
An idle hack & slash RPG where the adventure keeps going even while you're away.

Features

  • 🔮 The more it bounces, the stronger it getsTackle the Reflection Orb and its multiplier rises with every bounce off a wall. Build it up, then blast the whole horde in one hit.
  • ⏳ Progress even while idleYour hero keeps fighting while the app is closed — come back to a pile of coins.
  • 🐾 A colorful partySummon and train companions from N to UR. Their "Bond" and "Grace" abilities power up the whole party.
  • 🌌 Rebirth and go furtherStuck? Rebirth. Keep permanent bonuses and treasure chests, and push into deeper stages.
  • 🗼 Bosses, stage events and the Tower of TrialsMighty bosses at every milestone, surprise stage events like marching battle formations and moving gears, and the Tower of Trials to test your strength.
  • 🏆 RankingsAim for the top in best stage, max reflection multiplier and more.
